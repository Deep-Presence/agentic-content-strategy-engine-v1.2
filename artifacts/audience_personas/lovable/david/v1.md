# Audience Persona: IT Director at Mid-Market Manufacturing Firm

## 1. Persona Title & Snapshot

**Persona Title:** The Overwhelmed Guardian of Production Continuity

**Name & Snapshot:**

David is the IT Director at a $65M annual revenue automotive parts manufacturer employing 180 people across two facilities in the Midwest. With twelve years of manufacturing IT experience and a team of just three technicians supporting 220 endpoints plus aging SCADA systems, he faces relentless pressure to prevent the production downtime that costs his company $87,000 per hour while simultaneously defending against the ransomware attacks targeting 27.7% of all manufacturing cyberattacks. His purchase decisions accelerate sharply after near-miss security events or when insurance auditors flag vulnerabilities in his legacy infrastructure that hasn't been refreshed since 2017.

---

## 2. Daily Reality *(7 bullets)*

David monitors aging Windows Server 2012 instances supporting production line controllers that cannot be patched without scheduling costly downtime windows. He fields urgent Slack messages from the plant manager when the MES system freezes mid-shift, threatening $174,000 in lost production revenue. His mornings begin reviewing overnight security logs from firewalls protecting PLCs communicating over unencrypted MODBUS protocols with no authentication. He attends weekly executive meetings where the CFO demands IT budget justification while the COO complains that system updates disrupt production schedules. David spends afternoons writing RFPs for security vendors, navigating procurement approval matrices requiring CFO signoff above $25,000 expenditures. He manages vendor relationships with five different providers for ERP, SCADA maintenance, network infrastructure, cloud backup, and cybersecurity monitoring services. His evenings involve researching compliance requirements for SOC 2 audits that major customers now mandate before contract renewals.

---

## 3. Core Fears *(5 bullets)*

* Ransomware encrypting production systems during peak shipping season, halting all operations for 72+ hours
* Failed audit triggering insurance policy cancellation or premium increases exceeding $200K annually
* Unpatched vulnerabilities in decade-old industrial control systems being exploited by nation-state actors
* Production line shutdown blamed on "IT changes" destroying his credibility with operations leadership
* Critical vendor discontinuing support for legacy hardware with no budget approved for replacement

---

## 4. Deep Motivations *(4 bullets)*

* Achieve 99.5%+ uptime reliability that earns genuine respect from plant floor managers who currently view IT as obstacle rather than enabler
* Modernize infrastructure incrementally without triggering the production disruptions that ended his predecessor's tenure after a botched ERP upgrade
* Build defensible security posture preventing the catastrophic breach that would personally expose him to termination and industry reputation damage
* Secure executive approval for infrastructure investments by demonstrating clear ROI through prevented downtime costs and compliance risk mitigation

---

## 5. Trust Builders *(5 bullets)*

* Show me manufacturing-specific case studies documenting uptime improvements and prevented downtime costs, not generic enterprise testimonials
* Prove your solution integrates with our existing Rockwell PLCs, Wonderware SCADA, and Epicor ERP without requiring forklift infrastructure replacement
* Demonstrate you understand the difference between IT and OT security, with specific protections for legacy industrial protocols
* Provide transparent total cost of ownership breakdowns including implementation, training, ongoing licensing, and hidden integration expenses
* Offer phased rollout options allowing pilot testing in non-critical departments before touching production systems

---

## 6. Trust Killers *(5 bullets)*

* Promising "cloud-first" solutions requiring production system connectivity that violates our air-gapped OT security architecture
* Sales reps with zero manufacturing experience using generic IT talking points about "digital transformation"
* Requiring disruptive system downtime during our Q4 production peak for implementation or mandatory updates
* Vague security claims without SOC 2 Type II certification, penetration test results, or specific OT protocol safeguards
* Hidden costs discovered post-signature: per-device licensing, professional services minimums, mandatory annual maintenance escalators

---

## 7. Critical Pain Points *(7 bullets)*

* Managing cybersecurity for 40+ unsupported legacy endpoints running Windows 7 embedded in production equipment that cannot be upgraded without $2.3M capital expenditure approval
* Navigating procurement bureaucracy requiring three competing vendor quotes and CFO approval for any purchase exceeding departmental $15K authorization threshold
* Explaining to non-technical executives why "just patch it" recommendations from insurance auditors would halt production for sixteen hours
* Coordinating emergency response when third-party HVAC vendor's compromised laptop infects network during routine maintenance visit
* Maintaining compliance documentation for NIST, ISO 27001, and customer-mandated security audits with no dedicated compliance staff
* Preventing ransomware targeting manufacturing sector while defending SCADA systems designed without authentication or encryption capabilities
* Balancing IT security best practices against plant manager demands for contractor remote access and operational convenience

---

## How We Serve Them

While Lovable's core proposition centers on rapid full-stack application development for non-technical users, David represents a strategically valuable manufacturing IT segment where the platform addresses specific high-impact use cases without requiring production system integration. David's immediate needs focus on internal monitoring dashboards, compliance documentation systems, and departmental tools that reduce operational burden on his three-person team—exactly the "internal tool and dashboard development" use case where Lovable demonstrates proven capability. By enabling David to deploy production monitoring dashboards that visualize real-time equipment health, compliance audit trail systems, and vendor access request workflows without consuming scarce developer resources or triggering procurement approval thresholds below $25K, Lovable directly addresses his resource constraints while avoiding the production system integration risks that trigger his deepest fears.

The platform's value proposition aligns precisely with David's purchase triggers: after a near-miss security event exposes gaps in visibility across his manufacturing infrastructure, Lovable enables rapid deployment of monitoring and alerting dashboards that demonstrate proactive risk mitigation to insurance auditors and executive leadership. The GitHub code export addresses his vendor lock-in concerns, while the Supabase-backed authentication and audit logging satisfy baseline compliance documentation requirements. Critically, Lovable's isolated deployment model—building standalone web applications rather than integrating with legacy SCADA or PLC systems—sidesteps the air-gapped OT security architecture constraints and production disruption fears that disqualify most enterprise software. For David, Lovable represents a tactical productivity multiplier for non-critical internal tools, freeing his limited team capacity to focus on the mission-critical infrastructure maintenance that protects production uptime and prevents the catastrophic downtime events that define his professional survival.
